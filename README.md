<summary>🇬🇧 <b>ENGLISH (Main)</b></summary>
<br>

**Pulse Pad** is a lightweight background app that bridges Project Zomboid and your smartphone or tablet. It automatically hosts a local server, allowing you to view interactive maps and character statuses directly on your mobile device over Wi-Fi. 

> ⚠️ **Important:** Your PC and your smartphone/tablet **must be connected to the same Wi-Fi network** for the connection to work.
>
> ⚠️ **Disclaimer:** I am **not** the author of the original PZ Pulse and PZ Map mods. This utility is simply a convenience tool created to save players from manually configuring IP addresses.

### 🚀 Quick Start (iOS & Android)
The standard web version works flawlessly on **any device (Apple iOS or Android)** through your regular browser (Safari/Chrome).
1. Subscribe to the required mods in Steam Workshop:
   * [PZ Pulse (Status Panel)](https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=3753700423)
   * [PZ Map (Global Map)](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3770149036)
2. Go to the [Releases page](../../releases/latest) and download the latest `Pulse.Pad_vX.X.X_setup.exe`.
3. Launch the app (it automatically finds the mod paths).
4. Click **Start Server**, scan the QR code with your phone or tablet camera, and open the link.

### 📱 Optional: Native Android App
While the browser version works great for everyone, **Android users** can optionally grab our native companion app. It removes annoying browser borders, includes a built-in QR scanner, and keeps your screen awake while you play.
👉 **[Download Pulse Mobile for Android](https://github.com/Vlaseeds/Pulse-Mobile)** *(Not available for iOS).*

### 🛠 What if auto-detect fails?
If the app cannot find your mod folders automatically, don't worry. Simply launch Project Zomboid, open the mod settings in-game, click **"Copy dashboard URL"**, and paste that raw link directly into the Pulse Pad settings. The app will clean it up automatically.

### 🚀 Швидкий старт (iOS та Android)
